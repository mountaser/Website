About Me

I am someone who cannot leave a technical problem unresolved until it works seamlessly. Almost everything I know technically, I taught myself – because I wanted to build something that didn't exist yet: a custom Android system for a device the manufacturer had long abandoned, or a homelab server built from decommissioned hardware that grew into a full personal infrastructure.

Today that means: administering Linux and Windows servers, planning and securing networks, hosting containerized services, and automating builds via CI/CD pipelines. This is complemented by the hardware side – from soldering and microsoldering repairs to custom embedded electronics projects. What drives me is not just individual technologies, but understanding how a system truly works under the hood and having the persistence to achieve a clean solution.

In my Business Information Systems studies, I combine this technical depth with organizational structure: understanding requirements, documenting them clearly, and communicating complex technical concepts so they are easy to understand even without an IT background.
